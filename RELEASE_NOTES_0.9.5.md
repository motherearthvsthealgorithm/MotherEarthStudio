# Mother Earth Studio 0.9.5 — Story-First Foundation

## What changed

- Background footage now loops automatically for the full narration timeline.
- Every episode receives a 1.5-second intentional visual tail after narration.
- Music continues through the visual tail and fades out cleanly.
- Story-first caption generation reduces transcript segments to short emphasis phrases.
- A **Review Captions** button opens the generated SRT for human review before rendering.
- Caption placement samples the selected footage and chooses among five safe composition zones.
- Consecutive captions avoid repeating the same position when possible.
- Typography now prefers calmer serif fonts available on macOS (Didot, Hoefler Text, New York, or Georgia).
- Caption styling removes the heavy black box and uses a restrained outline and shadow.

## Important boundary

Story-first phrase selection in 0.9.5 is local and heuristic. It is intentionally reviewable rather than presented as perfect AI understanding. The creator remains the final editor.

## Upgrade

Copy the included files into the existing MotherEarthStudio_0.9.3 folder, replacing files when prompted. Your Assets, Output, settings, and virtual environment remain untouched.
